FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Will this void my warranty?

Feature configuration doesn't install aftermarket software. It changes settings your vehicle's modules already support. That said, a dealer could question a change related to a warranty claim. We back up your original settings so anything can be restored before a dealer visit.

Can you brick my module?

The risk is low when it's done right, and we work to keep it that way: full backups, verified battery voltage and no guesswork values. If a session ever leaves something worse than we found it, we'll make it right.

Which vehicles do you work on?

Ford, Lincoln, Mercury and Mazda vehicles that FORScan supports. That's most models from the mid-2000s onward. Include your VIN in the form and we'll confirm before you book.

What adapter do I need for a remote session?

A FORScan-compatible adapter with an HS/MS-CAN switch, such as OBDLink EX or vLinker FS. Cheap ELM327 clones often fail to reach every module. Tell us your vehicle and we'll point you to the right one.

Do I need my own FORScan license?

For remote sessions, yes. The free extended license works for most jobs and we'll help you set it up. For on-site jobs we bring our own.

What if it can't be done?

We confirm compatibility before booking. If something turns out to be impossible on your vehicle during the session, you don't pay for that job.
