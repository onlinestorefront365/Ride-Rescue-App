Supporting cast

The 17 service icons carry over unchanged in drawing and are recoloured: carbon on light, silver on dark, Electric Blue only for the active or selected state. Supporting graphics are cut from the emblem itself: the road line, the arc, the ridge and a gear quarter. They support the RR; none of them is ever used as a logo.
