Ride Rescue · Stage 3 · Iconography

Seventeen services, one drawing style

The brief's 12 roadside icons plus 5 for the diagnostics, programming and service work Ride Rescue sells today. 24-unit grid, 2-unit live-area padding, 2-unit stroke, round caps and joins, 2-unit corner radius. Vehicles face right (the direction of travel), and wheels are always r2 on y17. Icons inherit text colour.

Rules
  • Navy on light, lime or Soft White on navy. Never lime on light.
  • Use at 16, 20, 24, 32 or 48 px. Scale only the whole icon; never change the stroke.
  • One idea per icon. Badges (bolt, clock, plus) sit inside the live area.
  • New icons start from an existing one's vehicle, wheel or box so proportions stay shared.
  • Icons label services; they don't decorate. Pair each with a text label in UI.
