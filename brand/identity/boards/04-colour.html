Ride Rescue · Identity v2 · Colour

Carbon, steel, one electric signal

Your four colours are the brand. Six derived values (each mixed from those four) solve the two places the raw palette fails: white button text on Electric Blue, and anything silver on white. CMYK values are conversions. Electric Blue sits outside the CMYK gamut and prints duller, so vehicles and signage need a spot or vinyl match picked from a physical swatch.
