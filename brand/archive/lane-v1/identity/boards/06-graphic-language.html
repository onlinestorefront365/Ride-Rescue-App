Ride Rescue · Stage 3 · Graphic language

Everything comes from the lane

The supporting graphics are cut from the logo's own negative space and grid, so they reinforce the mark instead of competing with it. No stock car photos as a substitute, no racing stripes, and no checker or chevron patterns (see the emergency-vehicle note in Stage 1).

How to use them
  • Lane wedge: the logo's negative space, extracted. Use it large, always running off the top edge toward the horizon, in Navy Raised on navy (lime only at small sizes), as the one hero accent. Never more than one per layout, never rotated, never beside the logo at a similar size.
  • Road-marking pattern: staggered dashes on the 12-unit rhythm. Use it as a quiet background for app loading screens, social banners and the back of business cards, in navy-raised on navy only.
  • Lane divider: a tapered rule for section breaks and under headings. Navy on light, lime on dark.
  • Direction wedge (graphics/direction-wedge-*.svg): the wedge turned to point right. Use it for "next step" cues in place of generic arrows.
  • Cut-corner frame: panels and photo crops with the top-right corner clipped at 45°, with the cut equal to 2X at the panel scale. It works like the clipped corner of a road-sign plate. One per view.
